# Do this on your iPhone. Then stop.

You do not need to code. You do not need Docker.

## The only finish step

1. Open this link on your phone:
   https://github.com/lost6feetdown1330-ship-it/Shopify-Automation-2/settings/secrets/actions
2. If GitHub asks you to sign in, sign in.
3. Tap **New repository secret**.
4. Name: `SHOPIFY_ACCESS_TOKEN`
5. Secret: paste your automation token (the atkn_ one).
6. Tap **Add secret**.
7. Tap **New repository secret** again.
8. Name: `SHOPIFY_SHOP`
9. Secret: `bcyeb3-1s.myshopify.com`
10. Tap **Add secret**.

Then come back here and send the word: done

That is the whole finish. Grok does the rest.
